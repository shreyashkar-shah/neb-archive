A Note from the Developer

What I want you to know

While I was in high school, especially during exam season, I often struggled to find authentic and well-organized past papers. Most resources were scattered across different websites, outdated, or difficult to trust. That frustration inspired me to build NEB Archive—a centralized platform where students can easily access reliable question papers and study materials without wasting hours searching. This project is my attempt to solve the very problems I once faced as a student.

NEB Archive is still in its early days, and there’s plenty of room to improve. If you notice something missing, outdated, or broken, I’d genuinely appreciate hearing about it. Every suggestion and bug report helps make this platform better for every student who uses it.
